# CHAR9S

**Design & visual storytelling · Generative AI workflows · Reusable skills**

I’m an independent designer and builder with 15+ years in motion and visual design. My background spans film titles, CG content, brand identity, and running a creative studio—work that connects an initial idea to a finished deliverable.

That production background shapes how I approach AI: clarify the brief, organize the material, make deliberate creative choices, and review the result. My practice now includes generative imagery and video workflows alongside design and web projects.

On GitHub, I’m exploring a related question: how can useful working methods become reusable AI skills? I turn selected tasks into documented workflows with clear inputs, evidence checks, and review criteria. These projects are an extension of my design practice: making complex material easier to understand and work with.

## Career Evidence

**Make your experience easier for employers to understand.**

Career Evidence applies this approach to career communication: selecting relevant information, showing evidence, and presenting it clearly. It turns lessons from Canadian employment-service training materials and public employment guidance into practical AI workflows. The focus is straightforward: connect job requirements to real experience, explain contributions clearly, and keep every claim grounded in facts.

| Project | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Canada Resume Skill](https://github.com/CHAR9S/canada-resume-skill) | Tailor a resume, improve accomplishment statements, and understand each edit | Available |
| [Interview Presentation Skill](https://github.com/CHAR9S/career-presentation-skill) | Turn relevant experience into a personal introduction, project stories, and a focused interview deck | Available |

These are independently developed tools, not an institutional partnership or a promise of hiring results. My professional background is design and production; the career guidance is informed by training resources, not a claim of recruiting or career-counselling credentials.

Try the resume skill and share a fictional or redacted example in its [discussion forum](https://github.com/CHAR9S/canada-resume-skill/discussions). Follow along for future tools and experiments.

## 中文介绍

我是 CHAR9S，独立设计师与创作者，拥有 15 年以上动态与视觉设计经验，做过电影片头、CG 内容和品牌视觉，也经营过创意工作室。我的工作一直贯穿从最初想法到最终交付的过程。

这段制作经历也影响了我使用 AI 的方式：先弄清任务，整理素材，做出有依据的创作选择，再检查结果。目前的实践涵盖生成式图像与视频流程，以及设计和网站项目。

在 GitHub 上，我探索的是这段经验的另一种用法：把具体任务中的有效方法整理成可复用的 AI Skills，明确输入、步骤、事实依据和检查方式。设计与这些项目之间的联系，是让复杂的信息更容易理解，让工作方法更容易被使用。

**Career Evidence｜让真实经历，更符合加拿大招聘语境**

Career Evidence 将这套方法用于求职表达：筛选相关信息，展示真实证据，再组织成清楚的文字和演示。系列提炼加拿大本地就业服务机构的简历培训资料，结合公开就业指导，整理成可直接使用的 AI 工作流。围绕岗位要求、经历证据和成果表达，帮助求职者完成简历优化；配套的[个人求职／面试展示技能](https://github.com/CHAR9S/career-presentation-skill)也已发布。

项目强调本地申请习惯、具体岗位匹配、有依据的成果表达，以及能解释理由的修改。适用于新移民、毕业生、转行者和希望更清楚展示自身价值的专业人士。

本系列独立整理与开发，不代表资料来源机构，也不承诺面试或录用结果。欢迎使用、反馈和参与改进。
